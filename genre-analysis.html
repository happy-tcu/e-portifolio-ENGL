Genre and the Limits of Saying

A comparative analysis of UNESCO’s Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence and Abeba Birhane’s “Machine Learning and Coloniality.”

Two texts argue for adjacent things about global AI access — one in the harmonized prose of an international recommendation, the other in the named, evidence-driven idiom of a public lecture. The essay below uses Raelke Grimmer’s account of genre as a tool to read the two side by side and ask what each form makes sayable, and what it quietly forbids.
