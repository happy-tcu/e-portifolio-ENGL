Hi, I'm Happy.

Economics & Finance student building AI products and data systems for global impact.

I'm a sophomore at the M. J. Neeley School of Business and the John V. Roach Honors College at TCU, pursuing a B.S. in Economics and a B.B.A. in Finance with a minor in Strategic Communication. Before Fort Worth, I was a presidential leadership fellow at Bridge2Rwanda in Kigali — and that's where most of this story really starts.

I care about building tools that move resources to people who don't usually get them. That has looked like fundraising $160,000 to bring FIRST Lego League to 2,000+ Rwandan students, coaching a class of college applicants to $4.84M in scholarships, and now founding Bakame AI — a voice platform helping 2.9B English learners study in low-connectivity environments.
