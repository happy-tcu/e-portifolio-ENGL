Assignment 02

The AI Divide in the Global South

A case study of Rwanda — and the argument that AI access belongs in the human-rights conversation, not the development one.

The visual argument compressed this case onto a single poster; the research report is where the evidence lives in full. It tracks the gap between the AI conversation in the Global North — about overuse — and the conversation in the Global South, which is still about absence, and builds toward a hybrid model: a Global-South-led strategy with Global-North cooperation on terms set by the South.

Research report — ENGL 20803-010, Texas Christian University, 25 May 2026. Download the PDF.
