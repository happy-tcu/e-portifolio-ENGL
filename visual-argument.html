The AI Divide

Why the next human-rights conversation is about who gets to use artificial intelligence — a Rwanda case study.

In the Global North, the conversation about AI is about overuse — “brain rot,” addiction, displacement. In the Global South, the conversation is about absence. 2.6 billion people were offline in 2024, and Africa hosts effectively zero large-scale AI systems. The visual argument below makes the case that AI access is a human-rights issue, not a development question.
